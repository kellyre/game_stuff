Boolean Beekeeper

Rotate logic flowers, release the bees, and make the honey jars compute the right answer.

Learning mode: three calm steps before the honey math.

  1. Move the orange beekeeper cursor with arrows or WASD.
  2. Press Space/Z to rotate the selected lane or flower clockwise. X rotates backward.
  3. Press Enter or the Run swarm button. Yellow bees mean 1; cloudy jars mean 0.
