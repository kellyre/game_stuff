Gear Garden Almanac

Place brass gears, crank the greenhouse, and make clockwork flowers bloom at the exact right ratio.

Learning mode: a gentle tour of the greenhouse.

  1. Move the brass cursor with arrows or WASD. It visits pegs first, then the gear tray.
  2. Press Z or Enter to pick up a gear, place it on a peg, or swap with a placed gear.
  3. Press Space or the Test crank button. Meshed gears turn slowly; every flower reports what it got.
