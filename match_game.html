Spanish Matching Game

Match pairs on a board, or drill yourself with self-graded flashcards — same word lists, two ways to practice.

Word list

Need at least half the grid in pairs (8 for the default 4×4). If you give more, the round's pairs are drawn from them at random. Separate with a comma, =, tab, or - .

Grid size

Up to 6 × 9. One side must be even so the cards pair up. Wide grids get cramped on small screens.
